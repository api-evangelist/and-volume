# &Volume

&Volume is an AI-powered risk management platform for digital assets, built for institutional investors such as family offices, asset managers, and corporate treasuries. The platform normalizes data across exchanges, custodians, protocols, and strategies, then monitors risk in real time, compares uncorrelated yield and arbitrage opportunities, and rebalances capital among them with varying levels of AI-assisted automation.

Founded by a team drawn from Deutsche Bank, BNP Paribas, Jane Street, and EPAM, &Volume partners with the University of Edinburgh AI Laboratory and the LUMI supercomputer to build explainable, testable AI for capital orchestration as global assets move on-chain.
